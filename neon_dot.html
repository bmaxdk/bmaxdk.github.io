▸ Controls
← ↑ → ↓ Move Pac-Man through the maze Y Toggle AI agent (auto-play) M Mute SFX P Pause
▸ Rules
Eat all cyan dots to clear the level. Grab a power pellet to make ghosts vulnerable for a few seconds — chase and eat them for big bonus points. Avoid touching ghosts otherwise. Combo multiplier builds up while you eat without stopping; standing still or hitting a ghost resets it.
▸ AI Agent
Hit Y or click AI to engage the planning agent. It runs BFS distance maps from Pac-Man, scores each candidate intersection move with a value function combining food proximity, ghost danger penalty, pellet timing, and scared-ghost bounty. To plug in your own policy, set game.policy = (obs) => 'up'|'down'|'left'|'right' from the dev console.
